**cli:** `pixel ai-cli-readify` reports provider readiness for Codex, Claude Code, Antigravity and Devin. It probes Ollama Cloud and names the provider's own condition (a missing key, a 429, a 402, a gateway 400, a 404 model) instead of collapsing them into "unreachable". `--apply` points the agents' configs at the provider that answered, merging rather than replacing, and leaves Devin verified rather than rewritten. `--approve --workspace <dir>` clears that folder's own startup gate.
