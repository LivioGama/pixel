**cli:** `pixel classify` — deterministic zero-shot decision over a bounded label set: embeds the question and each option's criterion with the shared embedding model, then maps cosine similarities through a fixed-temperature softmax into a probability distribution. No LLM, no daemon, no index required; `--jsonl` serves one decision per stdin line with the model resident (the shape a Jev-class decision benchmark consumes) ([`#209`](https://github.com/LivioGama/pixel/pull/209))
