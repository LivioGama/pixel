**ultraflow:** `pixel ultraflow discover --url --goal` runs the browser loop with `pixel classify` deciding: one question per cycle whose options are the operation-target pairs the page offers, so one answer is one action. `--save` composes what worked into a `pixel replay-flow` document. `pixel ultraflow replay <name>` follows it, deciding its conditions with `pixel classify` rather than matched text; `--update` records a step it had to re-decide. A question is bounded by the engine's option budget (the local model accepts 64 labels, measured; a longer page reports the tail it left out).
