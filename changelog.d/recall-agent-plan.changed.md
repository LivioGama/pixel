**recall:** `recall search --agent` picks its plan from the turns the agent's sessions hold: past 20 000 it walks the time index and stops at the first page instead of sorting all of them first (`recall search . --agent claude --limit 50` on a 248k-turn corpus: 1.34 s to 11 ms; `--agent pi`: 190 to 19 ms). Under it the sort stays, so a small agent is never slowed by a full walk.
