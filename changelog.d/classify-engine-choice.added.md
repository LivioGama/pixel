**classify, install:** `pixel install` proposes the classify engine — local Ollaya (auto-setup: the `ollaya` binary into a pixel-managed prefix plus the recommended `winnow:e4b` model, on-demand daemon) or a remote LLM with a stored key (new `deepseek` and `opencode-go` presets) — and `pixel classify` needs no flags: the stored choice decides, probing the local daemon and falling back to remote.
