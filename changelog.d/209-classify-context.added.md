**cli:** `pixel classify --context` (JSONL key `"context"`) — the framing every candidate shares: the question being asked, the rubric preamble. It is embedded into each candidate instead of being concatenated onto the text, so it cancels in the comparison rather than diluting the state. The model mean-pools, so placement decides the answer: on the 231 public JevBench items, moving the shared instructions out of the text took chance-corrected Intelligence from 19.5 to 38.3 with no other change ([`#209`](https://github.com/LivioGama/pixel/pull/209))
